The rest of the rules

You know the big one: pick a team to win each round, and each team can only be used once all comp. Here's everything else.

01

Picks

Lock
First kick-off of the round (pool rounds: Friday 8pm). Change your pick as often as you like till then; the latest one counts.
Hidden
Nobody sees a pick before the lock, admin included. Every pick is revealed in the group at the lock.
Missed it
You get a random team you haven't used. Round 1: no pick, no entry.
02

Results

Pool stage
The 80-minute result. A draw puts you out.
Knockouts
Whoever goes through, extra time and kicking competition included.
Called off
Match postponed or abandoned: you survive and get the team back.
All out
If everyone left goes out in the same round, you all stay in.
No teams left
You're out. If everyone still in is stuck at the same lock, it's a clean slate for all.
03

Out? Two ways back

Buy-back

€10
One per person, pool stage only. Pay into the pot before the next round's lock and you're back in.
Last chance: the Round 4 lock, Fri 15 Jan, 8pm.

Resurrection

Free
Round 4 only. Pick an away team you haven't used: if it wins, you're back for the knockouts.
One or the other in Round 4, not both. Out in Round 4 itself? No way back.
The price
Used teams stay used, and you also lose the most-picked team from the round you went out in, plus one more for every round you sat out.
